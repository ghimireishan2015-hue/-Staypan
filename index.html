About Our Micronation

Staypan is a privately organized micronation project based in Pennsylvania. It celebrates the ideas of citizenship, self-governance, history and national identity through voluntary participation and shared traditions.

Staypan does not claim independent sovereign status. Its activities remain subject to applicable United States and Pennsylvania law.

Founding Principles

The Founding Declaration of Staypan

Article I — Identity
Staypan is a voluntary micronation project established to create a distinct civic and cultural identity.

Article II — Law
All participants remain subject to applicable United States, Pennsylvania and local laws.

Article III — Leadership
Staypan may establish its own internal offices, customs, ceremonies and governance rules.

Article IV — Rights
Participation is voluntary. No Staypan rule overrides the legal rights or obligations of any person.

Article V — Peaceful Purpose
Staypan pursues its activities peacefully and respects the rights of others.

This is a proposed founding charter, not a government-issued constitution or a legally recognized sovereign instrument.

Citizenship of Staypan

Become Part of the Project

Staypan may offer voluntary honorary citizenship, membership certificates and a register of participants.

Such citizenship is symbolic and does not grant nationality, a passport, immigration status or special legal privileges.
